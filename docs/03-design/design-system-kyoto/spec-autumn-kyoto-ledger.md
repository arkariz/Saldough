## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the serene, grounded atmosphere of Kyoto during late autumn—intertwining historic merchant ledger craftsmanship, baked kiln ceramics, and textured washi paper. It bridges organic artisanal warmth with exact architectural discipline.

### Design Archetype & Principles
- **Style Archetype:** Tactile Minimalist with warm structural precision. It replaces sterile, cool technology tones with sun-warmed clay, golden amber, and toasted biscuit tones.
- **Atmospheric Quality:** Grounded, meditative, quiet, and trustworthy. High-contrast harshness is avoided in favor of tonal harmony and warm natural balance.
- **Harmonious Warmth:** Rather than sharp chromatic splits or jarring cool greens, the palette centers on an unbroken autumnal spectrum: acorn ochre, soft baked terracotta, toasted honey biscuit, and warm sand.
- **Target Audience:** Modern financial tools, artisanal marketplaces, high-craft publishing platforms, and archival workspaces requiring calm focus, durability, and understated elegance.

## Colors

The color palette is calibrated around an authentic, warm late-autumn harmony. The secondary palette has been rebalanced into a soft, muted terracotta (`#b87352`) and warm biscuit tones, eliminating discordant deep-forest greens or harsh chromatic clashes with the primary amber.

### Palette Roles & Balance
- **Primary (`#b45309` - Roasted Acorn Amber):** The definitive focal tone. Used for decisive calls to action, active navigation states, primary key values, and critical interactive anchors.
- **Secondary (`#b87352` - Soft Muted Terracotta):** Softened and warmed to blend seamlessly with the primary amber. Used for secondary buttons, quiet interactive chips, structural supportive accents, and highlighted status badges without jarring eye fatigue.
- **Tertiary (`#c4883b` - Muted Honey Biscuit):** A warm, luminous autumnal accent for informational indicators, subtle warnings, and financial highlight treatments.
- **Neutrals & Ground Surfaces:**
  - `surface-canvas` (`#fbf9f4`): Warm washi base layer with ivory undertones.
  - `surface-container-low` (`#f6f1e8`): Recessed cards, grouped module wells, and ledger trays.
  - `surface-container` (`#f0eade`): Standard card backplates and panels.
  - `surface-container-high` (`#e7dfd1`): Elevated floating panels and popovers.
  - `text-primary` (`#2a2421`): Charcoal chestnut brown providing crisp contrast without harsh black.
  - `text-secondary` (`#5e544c`): Muted river stone for body metadata and secondary labels.
  - `text-tertiary` (`#857a70`): Gentle warm gray for disabled indicators and tertiary tags.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances architectural structure, humanist warmth, and tabular ledger discipline:

- **Headlines (Epilogue):** Provides strong structural weight and crafted editorial proportion. Used across page hero units, modal headers, and key informational dividers.
- **Body Text (Source Sans 3):** Clean humanist letterforms ensure high legibility across long-form reading and documentation, avoiding ink traps or visual fatigue on warm tinted surfaces.
- **Metrics & Technical Labels (JetBrains Mono):** Evokes the artisanal ledger aesthetic. Applied across data tables, timestamps, monetary figures, status codes, and section counters. Always pair financial tallies with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry takes cues from modular tatami proportions, using disciplined 4px and 8px rhythm blocks that maintain clear structure across devices.

### Breakpoints and Grids
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout. Margin `2.5rem` (`40px`), gutter `1.5rem` (`24px`). Max-width bounded at `1360px` to maintain comfortable optical horizontal lines.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Margin `2rem` (`32px`), gutter `1.25rem` (`20px`).
- **Mobile (0 - 767px):** 4-column layout. Margin `1rem` (`16px`), gutter `1rem` (`16px`). Fluid stacking with accessible tap targets (minimum 44px height).

### Spacing Principles
- Density favors structured, neat spacing over excessively sparse voids.
- Deliberate horizontal separators (`outline-subtle`) divide modules instead of massive blank vertical gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on warm tonal layering and soft ambient occlusions rather than heavy drop shadows or stark dark projections.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** `#fbf9f4`. Crisp, un-elevated base layer.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Cards & Containers):** `#f0eade` with a subtle 1px border of `#ded5c6`. Shadow: `0 1px 2px rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Hover & Active Cards):** `#f6f1e8` with 1px border `#d5c5b0`. Ambient drop shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(184, 115, 82, 0.05)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Drawers, Menus & Dialogs):** `#fbf9f4` framed by 1px `#c4b7a2`. Ambient cast: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.10), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft (Level 1)** structural posture. Radii are modest and disciplined, honoring crafted timber and folded paper rather than rubberized pill geometry.

- **Micro Controls (Badges, Checkboxes, Switches):** `0.125rem` (`2px`) to `0.25rem` (`4px`).
- **Inputs & Interactive Buttons:** `0.25rem` (`4px`).
- **Cards, Panels & Modal Containers:** `0.5rem` (`8px`).
- **Circular Radii:** Limited strictly to user profile avatars and status indicator dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#b45309` (Acorn Amber), text `#fbf9f4`. Hover states darken gently to `#9a4408` with a 1px baseline stroke of `#78350f`.
- **Secondary:** Background `#b87352` (Soft Terracotta), text `#ffffff`. Hover transitions smoothly to `#a36040`. Soft, warm, and natural alongside the primary amber without competing for dominance.
- **Tertiary / Outlined:** Surface `#f0eade`, 1px outline `#ded5c6`, text `#2a2421`. Hover shifts subtly to `#e7dfd1`.
- **Button Micro-Interactions:** 100ms cubic-bezier transition, translating 1px downward on click.

### Chips & Badges
- **Terracotta Soft Chip:** Background `#f3ded4`, border 1px solid `#e5bcab`, text `#5a2d18`. Provides low-stress categorical organization.
- **Amber Status Chip:** Background `rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.10)`, border 1px solid `rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.28)`, text `#b45309`.
- **Neutral Washi Chip:** Background `#e7dfd1`, border 1px solid `#ded5c6`, text `#2a2421`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Container:** Background `#fbf9f4`, border 1px `#8a7f74`, corner radius `0.25rem`.
- **Focus State:** Border smoothly shifts to `#b45309` with a gentle `0 0 0 2px rgba(184, 115, 82, 0.20)` ring. No harsh neon outlines.
- **Affixes & Labels:** Right-aligned units (`YEN`, `KG`, `QTY`) set in JetBrains Mono `0.75rem` (`label-md`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Size:** 16px × 16px with `2px` corners for checkboxes; circular for radios.
- **Unchecked:** 1.5px border `#8a7f74` on `#fbf9f4`.
- **Checked:** Filled with `#b87352` (Soft Terracotta) holding an ivory `#fbf9f4` check indicator. Harmonizes quietly with nearby amber highlights.

### Cards & Data Lists
- **Cards:** Crisp 1px `#ded5c6` frame encasing `#f0eade` surface. Section headers feature subtle monospace indicators (e.g., `[ 01 // METRICS ]`).
- **Ledger Rows:** Hairline border `#ded5c6` row dividers. Rows hover to `#f6f1e8` with zero jarring color shifts. Tabular numbers in JetBrains Mono right-aligned for instant scanning.
